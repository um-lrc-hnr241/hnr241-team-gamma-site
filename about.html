Our Story & Why We're Here

Born from a stern parental ultimatum, fueled by espresso and cardboard meeples, and dedicated to bringing people together over tabletop games.

Origin Story

The Mother of All Ultimatums

Back in 2024, co-founders Milo and Sam had a small problem: their personal board game collection had officially crossed 300 boxes. Living room walkways were barricaded with expansions for Catan, kitchen counters were buried under stacks of Ticket to Ride, and opening the front closet risked an avalanche of 20-sided dice.

One fateful Sunday afternoon, both of their mothers showed up unannounced, took one look at the tabletop obstacle course, and delivered a unified, non-negotiable decree:

“Either you two get real jobs and move these 300 boxes of cardboard out of this house by the end of the month, or we're donating the whole lot to the community thrift shop.”

Faced with the unthinkable horror of parting with their beloved collection, Milo and Sam did the only rational thing two broke, game-obsessed best friends could do: they signed a commercial lease, pooled every dime of their savings for an espresso machine and heavy wooden tables, and gave their game collection a permanent public home called Rook & Roll.

Our Pitch

Why Rook & Roll?

We built the kind of space we always wished existed when we were growing up:

  • Table Time is Always Free: Buy a latte, a soda, or a plate of warm pretzel bites, and you can play as long as you want. No hidden hourly table rental fees.
  • 300+ Curated Games: From 2-minute party titles like Sushi Go! to 4-hour epic campaigns, our shelves have something for every table and skill level.
  • Zero Rulebook Stress: Our friendly game hosts are always on the floor ready to teach rules, set up boards, and give custom recommendations.
  • Gamer-Friendly Food: Delicious snacks and hearty meals crafted to keep your energy up without leaving sticky or greasy residue on the game pieces.
